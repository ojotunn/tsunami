How it works

Running in under five minutes

Four steps. Not one of them asks for your private key.

Connect any wallet

Sign a plain-text message to prove the address is yours. Signing moves no funds and authorizes no transaction. You do not need to be the token's creator.

Point it at a token

Paste the contract of any pons token — yours or not. You get a brand-new dedicated wallet for the agent, encrypted with your own password. It can only move what you send it.

Fund it

Deposit only what you are willing to put to work, and withdraw it any time. If the token is yours, one extra signature also points your creator rewards at the agent.

Pick your functions and watch

Every proposal shows the reasoning, the price impact and the verdict from both checks. On the default setting, nothing moves without your approval.

Built for pons v2

From the bonding curve to the burn address, automatically

A pons v2 launch trades on a bonding curve and pays its creator through a fee escrow. The agent speaks both, and a v1 token still works exactly as before.

01 · CurveFees accrue on the curve

Every buy and sell on your launch charges a fee. Part of it is yours.

02 · EscrowSwept into the fee escrow

The agent sweeps the curve and claims your balance — no key of yours involved.

03 · AgentBuys on the curve

One transaction, ETH in, tokens out, at the price the contract itself quotes.

04 · BurnSent to 0x…dEaD

Bought tokens leave circulation. The dashboard shows the real supply.

After graduation a v2 token trades on Uniswap v4. Reward collection keeps working there; buying on the v4 pool is on the roadmap, and until then the agent says so instead of guessing.

Safety

Built like something that holds money

The rules below are not settings. They are how the code is written.

Your key never leaves your wallet

You sign a message to log in and, if you are the creator, one transaction to delegate rewards. That is all the app ever asks you to sign.

Nothing is sent without a simulation

Every transaction runs through the node first. If it would revert, it is not sent and no gas is spent.

Withdrawing is always open

Maintenance mode, live-execution lock, risk policy — none of them apply to taking your own money out.

Every fee is on screen before you approve

The service fee is charged in ETH only, shown per action, and never taken from your tokens. Withdrawals and airdrops are free.

FAQ

Questions people ask before depositing

Short answers. The long ones are in the terms.

Do I need to be the token's creator?

No. Buybacks, scheduled buys, dip buying and airdrops work for any pons token. Only creator-reward collection needs the creator's signature, because only the creator can hand those fees to the agent.

Where is the agent's wallet, and who controls it?

The agent gets its own wallet, generated on the server and encrypted with a password only you know. You can export the keystore or withdraw everything at any moment. It never has access to your main wallet.

Does anything happen without my approval?

By default, no. The agent proposes; you approve. You can raise the auto-approval threshold in the risk policy once you trust it — and lower it back any time.

Does burning change the total supply?

No. The pons token has no burn function, so burned tokens sit at 0x…dEaD forever. Total supply stays the same; the dashboard reports circulating supply separately so the number you publish holds up.

What does it cost?

Gas, plus a service fee shown on screen before every action, charged in ETH on what the agent spends or collects. Withdrawing is free.
