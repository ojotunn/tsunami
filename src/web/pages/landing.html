02

Running in under five minutes

Four steps. Not one of them asks for your private key.

Connect any wallet

You sign a plain text message to prove the address is yours. Signing moves no funds and authorizes no transaction. You do not need to be the token's creator — that only matters for collecting creator rewards.

Point it at a token

Paste the contract address of any token launched on pons — yours or not. You get a brand new, dedicated wallet for the agent, encrypted with your own password. It can only ever move what you send to it, never your main wallet.

Fund it

Deposit only what you are willing to put to work — and withdraw it any time. If the token is yours, one extra signature also points your creator rewards at the agent.

Pick your functions and watch

Every proposal shows the reasoning, the price impact, and the verdict from both checks. On the default setting, nothing moves without your approval.

None of this is financial advice. Fixed-supply tokens in a single pool are highly volatile. Buybacks and burns do not guarantee price — pons's own documentation says as much. Providing liquidity can lose more than the fees it earns.

Providing liquidity or running systematic buybacks may be a regulated activity depending on your jurisdiction and volume. Worth talking to a lawyer before you scale. If your token runs programmatic buybacks, disclosing that publicly is what separates a transparent treasury from a manipulation complaint.
